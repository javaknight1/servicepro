### [Glossary](./reference/glossary.md)

Common terms and status definitions used in ServicePro.

**Job Statuses:**

- Scheduled - Job is on the calendar
- In Progress - Technician is working
- Completed - Job is finished
- Cancelled - Job was cancelled

**Invoice Statuses:**

- Draft - Not yet sent
- Sent - Sent to customer
- Paid - Payment received
- Overdue - Past due date
