# Quick Start Guide

Get ServicePro up and running in 15 minutes.

## Before You Begin

- Your ServicePro login credentials (from welcome email)
- Company information (address, phone, logo)
- At least one customer to add

## Step 1: Log In

1. Go to **app.servicepro.com**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. Change your password on first login

## Step 2: Explore the Dashboard

| Area              | Purpose                          |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Top Navigation    | Access Customers, Jobs, Invoices |
| Dashboard Widgets | Quick stats on revenue and jobs  |
| Quick Actions     | Create jobs, customers, quotes   |
| Today's Schedule  | Upcoming appointments            |

## Step 3: Set Up Your Company

1. Click your profile icon > **Settings** > **Company Profile**
2. Fill in: Company Name, Address, Phone, Email
3. Upload your logo
4. Click **Save Changes**

## Step 4: Add Your First Customer

1. Click **Customers** > **+ Add Customer**
2. Fill in customer details:
   - Name, Type (Commercial/Residential)
   - Contact name, email, phone
   - Service address
3. Click **Save Customer**

## Step 5: Create Your First Job

1. Click **Jobs** > **+ New Job**
2. Select your customer
3. Fill in: Job Type, Description, Date, Time
4. Assign a technician (optional)
5. Click **Save Job**

## Step 6: Generate an Invoice

1. Open the completed job
2. Click **Create Invoice**
3. Add line items with prices
4. Click **Save & Send**
